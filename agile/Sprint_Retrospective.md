# Sprint Retrospective

## What Went Well

- The team structured the project around clear architecture domains.
- Concurrency risks such as double-booking were identified early.
- Documentation and diagrams remained aligned with the system design.

## What Was Challenging

- Balancing detail and scope was difficult because the project covers design, operations, testing, and infrastructure.
- Some deployment and operational artifacts were added later than ideal.

## What We Learned

- Queue-based and lock-based thinking is essential for ticketing systems.
- Operational documents such as incident management and postmortems add maturity to the project.
- Infrastructure artifacts should be planned alongside architecture, not only at the end.

## Action Items

- Validate every required submission artifact against the checklist.
- Keep document wording consistent across the final report, diagrams, and deployment files.
- If more time is available, add runnable examples for the backend skeleton and automated tests.
