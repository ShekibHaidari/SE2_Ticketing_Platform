# Product Backlog

## Backlog Overview

The product backlog is organized by epic to reflect the main architecture domains of the Event Ticketing Platform.

## Epics

| Epic ID | Epic Name | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Identity and Access Control | Support secure registration, login, authorization, and role management |
| E2 | Event Catalog and Discovery | Help customers search and discover events efficiently |
| E3 | Reservation Engine and Seat Locking | Prevent double-booking through reliable seat reservation logic |
| E4 | Checkout and Payment | Provide secure and traceable payment handling |
| E5 | Ticket Issuance and Notification | Issue tickets with QR codes and notify users reliably |
| E6 | Deployment, Monitoring, and Operations | Prepare the platform for scalable deployment and operational control |
